## 📌 Project Overview & Objectives

The primary goal of this project is to explore and analyze retail sales transactions to help the business optimize marketing campaigns, streamline inventory stocking, and improve customer segmentation.

### Key Business Questions Addressed:
1. **Gender Demographics:** Who drives higher purchasing volume and sales revenue—male or female shoppers?
2. **Age Group Segmentation:** Which age brackets contribute the largest share of overall revenue?
3. **Geographic Distribution:** Which top states generate the highest purchasing volume and revenue?
4. **Occupational Background:** Which employment sectors possess the highest consumer purchasing power?
5. **Product Category Performance:** Which product lines generate maximum revenue vs. order frequency?
6. **Marital Status Impact:** How does marital status influence transaction volume across genders?
