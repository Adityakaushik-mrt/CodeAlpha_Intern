# Superstore Sales Performance & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview

This project uses Python-based exploratory data analysis (EDA) and data visualization to examine Superstore sales, profit, discounting, customer segments, regional performance, and shipping-mode profitability. The notebook transforms transactional data into business-focused charts and a single-page executive dashboard.

The analysis is designed to help identify sales trends, compare product sub-category performance, understand the relationship between discounts and profit, and highlight differences across regions, customer segments, and shipping modes.

## Business Questions

The notebook explores these questions:

1. How do monthly sales and profit change over the 2022–2023 period?
2. Which product sub-categories generate the most sales and profit, and which produce losses?
3. How are discounts associated with transaction-level profit?
4. How does sales performance vary by region and product category?
5. What share of revenue comes from Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office customers?
6. Which shipping modes have the strongest profit margins?

## Analysis & Visualizations

### 1. Executive KPI Scorecard
Calculates and prints:
- Total sales (revenue)
- Total profit
- Overall profit margin
- Number of unique orders
- Average discount

### 2. Monthly Sales & Profit Trend
A time-series line chart compares monthly sales and profit across the period represented in the data.

### 3. Product Sub-Category Performance
A horizontal bar chart compares sales and profit by sub-category to help distinguish high-volume products from less-profitable product lines.

### 4. Discount vs. Profit Analysis
A regression scatter plot visualizes the relationship between transaction discount rates and profit, with a zero-profit reference line.

### 5. Regional Sales by Category
A grouped bar chart compares sales for Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology across the West, East, Central, and South regions.

### 6. Customer Segment Revenue Share
A donut chart shows the percentage contribution of Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments to total sales.

### 7. Profit Margin by Shipping Mode
A bar chart compares profit margins across shipping modes using total profit divided by total sales for each mode.

### 8. Executive Dashboard
Combines six visual views into one 3 × 2 dashboard and saves the output as:

`superstore_executive_dashboard.png`

## Key Findings Documented in the Notebook

The notebook's written interpretation highlights the following findings. These should be revalidated against the exact dataset and refreshed notebook outputs before being used for business decisions:

- Higher discount rates are associated with weaker profitability; the notebook identifies discounts above 20% as a potential loss-risk area.
- Product profitability differs by sub-category: the notebook identifies Copiers and Machines as strong performers and Tables as a loss-making area.
- The notebook highlights the West and East regions for Technology sales.
- Consumer customers are described as contributing more than 54% of revenue, with Corporate customers at approximately 30%.
- Same Day and Standard Class shipping are described as achieving healthy margins in the analysed data.

## Recommendations Listed in the Notebook

- Review discount policies, particularly for furniture products such as Tables.
- Align inventory planning with regional sales and product profitability.
- Review shipping-mode profitability when planning fulfillment options.

These are notebook interpretations, not universal rules. Validate the findings with current data, sample size, and relevant business context before taking action.
